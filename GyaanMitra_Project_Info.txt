# GyaanMitra Project Overview

## What is GyaanMitra?
GyaanMitra (Knowledge Friend) is an AI-powered study assistant. It helps students plan their studies, stay focused with timers, and track their progress using machine learning.

## Core Features
1. **AI Study Planner**: Automatically creates a daily timetable so you know exactly what to study and when.
2. **AI Advisor**: Tells you if your study plan is realistic. If you are falling behind, it suggests how many extra hours you need to study to finish on time.
3. **Smart Timer**: A focus timer (Pomodoro style) with alarms to help you study without distractions.
4. **Flashcards**: Helps you memorize topics by creating digital question-answer cards.
5. **Notes**: A simple way to keep track of important study points.
6. **Quiz & Progress**: Tracks your quiz scores to show how much you are improving.
7. **Syllabus Parser**: You can upload a PDF syllabus, and the AI will automatically extract the subject and chapter names for you.

## Tech Stack (The Tools Used)
- **Frontend (UI)**: React.js, Vite, Material UI (for buttons/layouts), Chart.js (for graphs).
- **Backend (Server)**: Node.js, Express.js.
- **Database**: MySQL (using Sequelize to manage data).
- **AI/ML**: Python, FastAPI (to run the AI), Scikit-learn (for predictions).
- **APIs**: Google Gemini (to understand text) and OpenAI/LM Studio.

## How the AI & Machine Learning Works
- **The Model**: We use a "Random Forest" model in Python.
- **What it Predicts**: It predicts the **Priority** of a subject (High, Normal, or Low).
- **How it Predicts**: It looks at 5 things:
  1. How hard the subject is (Difficulty).
  2. How many days are left until the exam.
  3. Your past scores in that subject.
  4. How much syllabus you have already finished.
  5. Your daily available study hours.
- **Prediction Flow**: 
  User Inputs Data -> ML Model Calculates Priority -> Scheduling Engine Creates Slots -> AI Advisor Checks Feasibility.

## How the Study Planner Works (The Flow)
1. **Adding Subjects**: You enter your subjects, exam dates, and number of chapters.
2. **Setup**: You tell the app how many hours you can study each day and your preferred study times.
3. **Generation**: The app sends this to the ML model. The model decides which subjects are most urgent.
4. **Timetable**: It creates "Time Slots" (e.g., 10:00 AM - 11:30 AM: Physics Chapter 1). It ensures no two subjects overlap.
5. **The Warning System**: If you have too much to study and not enough time, the "AI Advisor" shows a warning and tells you to increase your daily study hours.

## Database Tables (Where Data is Saved)
1. **users**: Saves your name, email, password, and settings.
2. **Flashcards**: Saves your study questions and answers.
3. **Notes**: Saves your sticky notes.
4. **study_sessions**: Records every time you use the focus timer.
5. **quiz_results**: Saves your scores after you take a quiz.
6. **planner_subjects**: Saves the subjects you added to your planner.
7. **planner_sessions**: Saves the actual daily timetable blocks created for you.
8. **planner_recommendations**: Saves the advice and warnings given by the AI Advisor.

## Project Flow (How everything connects)
- **User clicks "Generate Plan"** -> **Frontend** sends data to **Backend** -> **Backend** asks the **ML API** for a plan -> **ML API** calculates the best schedule -> **Backend** saves it to **MySQL** -> **Frontend** displays the timetable and AI advice.
